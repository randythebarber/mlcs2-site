Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Major League Cuts Studio 2, 387 Valley Road, Clifton, NJ 07013 (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit www.mlcs2.com, book with us, or get messages from us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, phone number, email address, the service and barber you choose, your appointment times, and any notes you add.
  • Payment details: card payments are handled by Square. We see things like the amount and the last four digits of the card, never the full card number.
  • Messages: what you send us by phone, text, email or social media.
  • Website use: basic technical information that any website receives, such as your browser type, device and the pages you visit.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you of and run your appointments.
  • To take payments and keep business records.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To send offers and shop news, only if you agreed to receive them.
  • To keep the website working and improve it.

Square

Our booking, payments, customer list and loyalty program run on Square (Block, Inc.). Square processes your booking and payment details for us and under its own privacy notice. The booking box on our Book page is provided by Square and may use Square’s own cookies.

Texts and emails

We send marketing texts or emails only if you agree. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with anyone else for their own marketing. We share it only with the service providers that send messages for us (such as Square), and only to send our messages.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the shop, such as Square (booking and payments), our website host, and email or analytics providers;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our clients, staff or business.

Cookies and analytics

Our website does not use advertising cookies of its own. We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful; it uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed and general location. Google’s use of that data is covered by Google’s privacy policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The Square booking box may set its own cookies, as noted above.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet tax and legal requirements. Ask us if you want your details removed and we will do so where the law allows.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can stop marketing messages at any time as described above. Contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. A parent or guardian books and gives the details for a child’s haircut.

Security

We use trusted providers and limit who can see client details. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
