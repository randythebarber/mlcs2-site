Just starting out

New to barbering? Start here.

Starting out is the hardest part. If you are a beginner and having a tough time finding a barbershop that will give you a chance, talk to us. We teach, we mentor, and we help new barbers build real-world confidence behind the chair.

Your educators

Randy and Kaly.

We are Randy and Kaly, barbers and trainers with over 10 years in the men’s grooming industry. We have done it all. Our journey began behind the chair, mastering cuts, color and styling, and quickly turned into a mission to share those skills with others.

As trainers, we have mentored aspiring barbers and stylists in workshops, shops and academies, helping them build strong foundations and real-world confidence. Education, to us, is just as important as execution, because great hair starts with great technique.

We stay current with global trends, always learning and teaching the latest. Whether you are sitting in our chair or joining one of our classes, expect creativity, precision and a deep commitment to excellence.

In the classes

What we teach.

  • Precision fades

  • Modern color

  • Styling and hair care

  • Life behind the chair

    Listening first, creating second, and building a clientele.
